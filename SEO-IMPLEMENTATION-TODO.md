# Sienna Shea Butter — SEO & discoverability plan

This is the working list distilled from the Claude and Gemini audits. It separates
safe website work from items that need an owner decision or account access.

## Ready for founder review — website release

- [x] Protect the private collection address: show Eco Park, Centurion and confirm
      the exact address only after a WhatsApp arrangement.
- [x] Correct public business hours to Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 and make EFT and
      cash payment options clear before a customer messages.
- [x] Keep the product and organisation information accurate for search engines:
      canonical Instagram URL, founder identity, South Africa service area and
      current product facts. Do not add an inaccurate public address, delivery price,
      return policy, baby-safety claim, or medical claim.
- [x] Cut mobile image payload: modern responsive product/community images, lazy-load
      non-critical gallery images, and preload only the main product image.
- [x] Prepare technical URL hygiene: redirect www and trailing-slash variants to the
      preferred canonical URL where host configuration permits.
- [x] Add distinct WhatsApp order messages for the homepage, product, delivery and
      guide pages so the source of an enquiry is visible inside WhatsApp.
- [x] Add a concise source/measurement note to the project documentation.
- [x] Test the final production build, mobile and desktop rendering, gallery motion, metadata,
      sitemap, robots, canonical URLs and key order links.

## Needs founder confirmation before publishing

- [ ] Returns/exchange policy for an unopened cosmetic product: return window,
      return method, who pays return courier, and treatment of damaged/wrong orders.
- [ ] Final shipping policy: whether "from R120" is the only public promise, plus
      usual handling times and whether collection remains available by arrangement.
- [ ] Whether to display a business email publicly in addition to WhatsApp.
- [ ] Any bundle price or multi-tub offer.

## Needs account access or a person outside the website

- [ ] Verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the
      sitemap, then enable IndexNow.
- [ ] Add analytics only after choosing a provider and accepting its privacy/cookie
      implications. Track WhatsApp click events.
- [ ] Keep the Google Business Profile as a service-area profile; confirm hours,
      service areas, cover photo and product once Google finishes processing them.
- [ ] Invite only real customers to leave honest Google reviews. No gifts, discounts,
      keyword scripts or review-gating.
- [ ] Build credible third-party corroboration: Instagram, local salons/barbers,
      independent creator reviews and retailer/marketplace applications.

## Content drafts — publish only with founder review

- [x] Shea butter for low, medium and high porosity hair.
- [x] Shea butter for relaxed hair.
- [x] LOC/LCO layering with shea butter.
- [x] Highveld winter hair care.
- [x] The realistic monthly cost of natural-hair care in South Africa.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim Sienna treats alopecia, eczema, hair loss, scalp conditions, stretch
  marks or dark marks.
- Do not make broad baby/toddler-safety claims for a formula containing essential and
  fragrance oils. Keep the patch-test and sensitive-skin guidance.
- Do not publish the home collection street address in Google Business Profile or
  structured data.
- Do not mark Sienna-controlled testimonials as Review or AggregateRating schema.
